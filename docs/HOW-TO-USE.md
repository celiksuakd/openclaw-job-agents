# How to use JobSquad

JobSquad is your job-search helper. It lives on your Mac and talks to you in a chat.

## Opening the chat
Double-click **JobSquad** on your Desktop. Your browser opens the chat.

## Every weekday morning
Around 08:00 you get a notification, and new matches appear in the chat:

```
#14 · 88 · Data Analyst — Acme · Remote, Europe · €50–60k
   SQL and dashboards every day, e-commerce; no Looker experience
   https://…
```
- `#14` is the job number.
- `88` is how well it fits you, out of 100.
- The line under it says why it fits, and what's missing.

## What you type
| You type | What happens |
|---|---|
| `approve 14` | JobSquad writes a CV and cover letter for this job and applies. You get a ✓ when it's sent. |
| `approve 14 17 20` | Same, for several jobs. |
| `skip 15 too junior` | Not interested. Giving a reason helps JobSquad learn what you like. |
| `show 14` | More about the job. |
| `status` | How the search is going. |
| A link to a job you found | JobSquad adds it, and applies if you say so. |
| `I have an interview at Acme on Tuesday!` | It updates your list (and cheers you on). |
| `also look in Berlin` / `no sales jobs` | It changes your search. |
| `pause` / `resume` | Stops or restarts the search. |

You can always write normally, in your own words.

## "Needs you" messages
Sometimes a company's site needs something only you can do: create an account, solve a "prove you're human" puzzle, or answer an unusual question. JobSquad sends you the link and tells you exactly what's needed. Everything else is already filled in.

## Promises JobSquad keeps
- It never sends an application you didn't approve (unless you turned on "apply on its own").
- It only uses facts from your CV and your own answers. It never makes anything up.
- It never creates accounts, uses passwords, or pays for anything.
- It keeps a copy and a screenshot of everything it sends, in **JobSquad Files → applications**.

## Every Friday
You get a short weekly report: how many jobs were checked, what was sent, and one or two suggestions to improve your results.
